# Correction ledger, Paper 3B

Every error found in this project's documents or code is recorded here, with how it was
found and what was changed. Entries before the freeze are design-stage corrections: no study
data existed when they were made. Numbering continues the program's practice (Paper 1: B-numbers,
Paper 2: continued B-numbers, Paper 3A: P3A-n).
